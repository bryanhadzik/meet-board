State qualifying times

The meet board shows last season's state cut for the event in the water, for the classification of each team in the heat, and tags every swimmer whose finish time beats their school's cut. UHSAA has no fixed cuts — each class takes its 32 fastest per event (20 relays) — so the built-in times are the slowest seed that made the 2026 state psych sheet.
