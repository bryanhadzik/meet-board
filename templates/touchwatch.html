Touch watch

Each lane's touchpad: armed it will take a touch, disarmed the console ignores it for a while after every touch (so a relay swimmer getting out doesn't count), done finished. It sounds an alarm when a lane's touch looks missed - the camera saw a touch with no split, or a touch is well overdue for the swimmer's pace - so the timers' watches get checked. An aid only: it never changes a time.
