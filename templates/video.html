Lanes
top row is lane bottom row is lane

Click the finish wall then the turn wall: on each, click where the 9 lane lines meet the wall — the lane-1 pool edge first, then each lane rope in order, then the lane-8 edge. Use the mouse wheel to zoom in for the far lanes.

Scoreboard: click the top-left corner of the top row's time, then the bottom-right corner of the bottom row's time (just the time digits, not the lane numbers). Zoom in with the mouse wheel. Then say which lanes the top and bottom rows are - rows cut off by the edge of the picture don't count.

Strobe: click two corners of a small box around the starter's strobe light (where it flashes). Keep the box tight so people walking past don't set it off. Best done on a recording paused on the flash.

Check the detection

Pick a heat, then click a lane's touch time to jump there in the video. The shaded boxes are where it watches for the touch; a lane's box flashes when the video reaches that lane's touch. When the guess is off, step to the first frame the hand is on the wall and click that lane's Set touch here - your marks are kept and used to tune the detection. Step frame by frame (1/30 s) to see the hand on the wall - or use the keys: ← → one frame, Shift+← → one second, space to play/pause.
